🇺🇸 America 250

Freedom Song

A musical journey through American history, freedom and the unfinished promise of equality.

🇺🇸AMERICA 250 — FREEDOM SONG⌄

Overture

From the shores of fourteen ninety-two... To the slave ships of sixteen nineteen... From forgotten nations... To cotton fields and auction blocks... From the waters of the Zong... To the footsteps of dreamers arriving on distant shores... From cracked ceilings... To towers touching the sky... Five years of solitary tracking. One thousand seven hundred seventy-six puzzles to traverse. Ten languages. Millions of separated families. One story. One human family. Mother Balamani, your words remain: "Always be a Good Samaritan."

Verse 1

In fourteen ninety-two the ships crossed oceans wide, Searching for a world unknown beneath the endless sky. Upon the fields and forests lived the first forgotten souls, Whose songs were carried by the wind before the story told. Then walls were drawn by power, And old paths disappeared. A nation rose from promises, But also from its tears. The drums of change kept beating, Yet echoes still remain. For every dream that's written down, Another bears the pain.

Pre-Chorus

Can you hear the mothers crying? Can you hear the fathers call? Can you hear the chains of history Rattling through us all?

Chorus

What have we done with the world we created? Can every child sleep safe tonight? Take down the walls that divide the human family, Let kindness be our light. One human race. One beating heart. One love beneath the sky. No more violence. No more fear. Let every dream survive.

Verse 2

From distant shores they carried them In sixteen nineteen's tide. Packed inside the wooden ships Where hope itself could hide. Families torn on auction blocks, Their names erased by trade. Mothers wept and fathers prayed As children were conveyed.

Verse 3

The years kept turning onward Through the battles and the scars. Till voices marched for justice Beneath the southern stars. A dreamer spoke of freedom. Millions heard the call. That liberty means little Until it reaches all.

Bridge I — The Dreamer

His mother spoke of sacrifice. His future had to rise. She would sell her treasured gold To keep alive his skies. Across the seas he journeyed. Where towers touched the blue. Searching for tomorrow. Like millions came to do.

Bridge II — The Family

One September she arrived. Carrying hope ashore. Believing life would open A brighter future's door. A son was born. A miracle. A light the darkness knew. A father saw tomorrow Looking back at him anew.

Spoken Interlude

One thousand seven hundred seventy-six puzzles. Ten language systems. A father's vault of memory. Built not from gold. But from persistence. Built not from power. But from love.

Bridge III — The Legacy

No thousand rupees for books. No palace made of stone. Just a cracked ceiling overhead. And dreams to call his own. The years became equations. The questions became keys. Searching through the stories Hidden in history.

America 250

The centuries kept turning, Through every rise and fall. Through broken chains and freedom dreams, A nation carried all. From wooden ships on distant seas, To rockets in the sky, Two hundred fifty birthdays passed, As generations journeyed by.

Final Chorus

What have we done with the world we created? Can every child sleep safe tonight? No more chains. No more shadows. No more stolen human rights. One human family. One human story. One hope for every land. Let compassion be our language. Let love be our command.

Outro

For the living. For the dying. For the weak. For the strong. For the mothers. For the fathers. For the children yet unborn. One human family. Carrying on. One human family. Carrying on. One human family... Forever carrying on.
